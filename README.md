## What it does

`main.py` (Python standard library only, no network, deterministic) writes a single-sentence pitch for a ridiculous invention that fixes a tiny everyday annoyance to `entry.txt` in the directory named by the `MAKEFUN_OUTPUT_DIR` environment variable (default `/output`).

The pitch: a tiny parachute clipped to your phone that senses the exact moment you fall asleep scrolling in bed and pops open to lower the phone gently onto the pillow instead of onto your face.
